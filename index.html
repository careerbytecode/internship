🚀 The Program

An internship that throws you straight into the work

CareerByteCode's DevOps Internship drops you into real AWS and Azure environments: the exact patterns engineering teams ship every day, handed to you as scenarios with services to use, evidence to produce, and a senior engineer waiting to review what you submit.

No screencasts. No copy-along tutorials. You read the brief, you build the solution, you prove it works.

📦 What You'll Ship

Every project is a slice of a real production system

A few examples of what you'll build, and have working in your own cloud.

CI/CD · Identity
A passwordless GitHub-to-Azure pipeline that deploys a containerized API on every push, no stored secret in sight.
Networking
A hub-and-spoke Azure network with segmented spokes and centralized egress, the way enterprises actually carve up a subscription.
GitOps · Kubernetes
A multi-environment GitOps setup on EKS with ArgoCD, including sync waves and automated rollback.

A supply chain that scans images, generates an SBOM, signs with cosign, and refuses to deploy anything that fails the gate.

A FinOps gate that posts the cost impact of every Terraform PR before it merges.

A cluster backup and restore flow with Velero, so you can prove disaster recovery actually works.

❓ FAQ

Questions, answered straight

When does it start and how do I register?
The internship starts June 15, and applications are open now. Register by messaging us on WhatsApp: apply here →
How long is the internship?
It runs over 3 months, self-paced. Most projects are two to six hours of active work each, so you set the rhythm and fit it around your week.
Is this remote?
Yes. The whole program runs online. You work from wherever you are, in your own cloud accounts, on your own schedule.
Can I do this alongside a job?
Most candidates do. Projects are two to six hours of active work each, and you set the pace.
Are the projects AWS or Azure?
Both. The catalog leans Azure, with a solid block of work on AWS, so you build real depth on Azure while still shipping on AWS.
What does the cloud cost?
Small. Most projects come in at cents to a few dollars each if you tear down the same day. You set a budget alert on day one as part of the program. Cluster-based projects run a bit higher, and we show you how to share one cluster across several projects to keep the total down.
Do I need to be an expert to apply?
No. You need Linux, Git, a terminal, and a working understanding of one cloud. Depth gets built inside the program, not before it.
What if I get stuck?
Our team is on hand. A senior engineer will help you unblock without solving it for you, the way a good lead does in a real job.
How do you decide a project is done?
Each project ships with a concrete Definition of Done: specific command output, a responding service, a tagged image, a passing scan, a clean teardown. You produce the evidence, we verify it.
What do I get at the end?
A GitHub portfolio of working production patterns and the proof you completed them. We don't promise placement, but we set you up with work you can actually defend in an interview.
Do I keep the code I write?
Yes. It lives in your own GitHub from day one.
What's the program fee?
We keep it student-friendly and flexible. Message us on WhatsApp and we'll share the current pricing and any ongoing offers. Ask us →
